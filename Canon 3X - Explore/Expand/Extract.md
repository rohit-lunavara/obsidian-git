> # Canon 3X: Explore/Expand/Extract
>
> Kent Beck presents a framework for understanding the three phases in the life of a successful idea:
>
> - **Explore:** Search experimentally for a new growth loop—the mechanism that makes the idea grow. Use rapid, creative experiments with tiny teams, minimal dependencies, and low-cost failures. The phase ends when one experiment performs significantly better than the others.
> - **Expand:** Scale the successful idea while overcoming one growth bottleneck after another. Focus intensely on the most important emerging obstacle, discard nonessential features, and use solutions that are sufficient for the moment rather than permanently perfect. The phase ends when growth becomes predictable.
> - **Extract:** Sustain growth profitably through safe optimization. Run small experiments, adopt successful improvements, and reduce costs. The phase ends when additional investment produces no meaningful return.
>
> Each phase requires a different approach to finance, team size, project management, personnel, technology, risk, implementation, marketing, and sales. Applying the methods of one phase to an idea in another phase can kill the idea.
>
> The broader lesson is that exploration, expansion, and extraction have different goals and risks: exploration seeks an uncertain breakthrough, expansion pursues rapid scaling, and extraction optimizes a mature system. Resources generated by one successful curve can then be used to explore new curves. [newsletter.kentbeck](https://newsletter.kentbeck.com/p/canon-3x-exploreexpandextract)
